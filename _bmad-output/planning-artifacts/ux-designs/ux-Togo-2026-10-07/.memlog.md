- (decision) Visual Direction: Direzione C (High-Contrast Utility) - crisp borders, high contrast for grocery aisle readability, clear quantity badges, generous one-handed touch targets
- (decision) Check-off behavior: Option B - struck items move to a collapsible 'Presi (N)' section at the bottom of the list, easily uncheckable on the fly before ending the trip
- (decision) Voice input interaction: slender bottom sheet slides up, transcribes spoken phrase in real time, displays structured parsing (product, qty, unit, category), explicit quick confirm button, no silent mutations
- (decision) Navigation Structure: Option 2 - Fullscreen active list with Storico (History) icon in top App Bar; maximum vertical space, clean single-surface ergonomics
- (decision) Conclude shopping trip flow: items remain crossed out in the session until explicit 'Concludi spesa' action. Checked items move permanently to History; unchecked items stay in the active list

Le parti vuote sono disabilitate o omesse; se entrambe vuote, l'icona mostra un messaggio e non apre il dialogo. Default sulla pagina corrente. Export = testo condiviso, non file.
